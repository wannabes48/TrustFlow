TrustFlow, a simple web application that provides an AI-driven alternative credit score for informal workers based on their mobile money transactions.<br>

The system allows users to seamlessly paste their transaction data and instantly gives them:<br>

1. A calculated Trust Score (0-100) based on total income, spending vs. income balance, and transaction consistency.<br>

2. A clear Risk Level indicator (Low, Medium, High) and suggested Loan Eligibility.<br>

3. Personalized Savings Recommendations generated by AI based on their unique financial habits.<br>

4. Visual Analytics comparing monthly income versus expenses to help users easily understand their financial flow.<br>

Powered by Gemini AI, the solution evaluates income stability and spending patterns, turning raw data into actionable financial insights for those who might not have a traditional credit history.<br>

I have leveled up TrustFlow based on your suggestions to make it much more accessible and actionable for informal workers. Here are the key improvements:<br>

1. Frictionless Data Ingestion<br>
SMS Text Parsing: Users can now simply copy and paste a block of their raw M-Pesa SMS messages into the text box. The app uses Gemini AI to automatically parse the raw text, extracting the Date, Amount, and IN/OUT flow.<br>
Demo-Data Toggle: I've added a prominent "+ Load Sample M-Pesa Data" button right under the text area so anyone can instantly see how the app works without needing their own data.<br>

2. Actionable Functional Features<br>
AI Expense Categorization: When parsing the SMS messages, Gemini now categorizes the "OUT" transactions based on the recipient's name (e.g., "Utilities", "Groceries", "Business Inventory").<br>
Expense Pie Chart: A new pie chart is displayed next to the bar chart, breaking down the user's spending habits visually.<br>
Downloadable Trust Certificate: Added a "Download My Score (PDF)" button. This uses html2canvas and jspdf to generate a clean, one-page PDF summarizing their Trust Score, risk level, and financial summary, which they can share with micro-lenders.<br>
Goal-Oriented Savings Prompts: The AI prompt has been updated to provide highly specific, goal-oriented savings recommendations based on their actual cash flow.<br>

3. Trust & Usability (Non-Functional)<br>
"Zero-Data Retention" Privacy Badge: A highly visible, reassuring banner is now displayed at the top of the app stating: "Privacy First: Your data is analyzed instantly and never saved or stored on our servers."<br>
Progressive Web App (PWA): I've added a manifest.json and a basic service worker (sw.js), allowing users to "Add to Home Screen" on their mobile devices for a native app feel.<br>
Localization Toggle: Added a simple EN/SW toggle in the header to switch the UI between English and Swahili instantly.<br>

4. UI/UX Polish<br>
Micro-interactions: The loading state now shows a spinner with the message "AI is analyzing your financial flow..." to keep the user engaged while Gemini processes the data.<br>
Color-Coded Feedback: The Trust Score and Risk Level are now strictly color-coded (Green for Low Risk / High Score, Amber for Medium, Red for High Risk) for instant visual comprehension.<br>
The app is now much more robust and user-friendly<br>

# Run and deploy your AI Studio app

This contains everything you need to run your app locally.

View your app in AI Studio: https://ai.studio/apps/4622406e-fc1b-4c19-ab4f-c7d0e2513802

## Run Locally

**Prerequisites:**  Node.js


1. Install dependencies:
   `npm install`
2. Set the `GEMINI_API_KEY` in [.env.local](.env.local) to your Gemini API key
3. Run the app:
   `npm run dev`
